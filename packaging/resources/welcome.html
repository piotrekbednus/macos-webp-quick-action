Convert images to WebP and AVIF from Finder

This adds two items to Finder's right-click menu: Quick Actions → Convert to WebP and Convert to AVIF. Select one image or fifty, right-click, and compressed copies appear next to the originals.

  • Runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Never changes or overwrites a file. The copy gets a new extension, or a number if that name is taken.
  • Keeps the colour profile, so Display P3 screenshots do not look washed out.
  • Removes camera and location metadata.

Installs for all users of this Mac. You will be asked for your password.

Free and open source (MIT). Includes the cwebp and avifenc encoders (libwebp, libavif, aom), which are under their own BSD-style licences.
