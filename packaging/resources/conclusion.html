All done

  1. In Finder, select one or more images.
  2. Right-click and choose Quick Actions → Convert to WebP or Convert to AVIF.
  3. A notification tells you when it is finished.

Not in the menu? Relaunch Finder (Option + right-click the Finder icon in the Dock → Relaunch), or turn the actions on in System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts → Services.
